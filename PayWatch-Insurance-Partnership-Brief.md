## Who we are

PayWatch is a personal-finance app for India that gives everyday people a private "financial CFO." We consolidate a user's salary, savings, investments, loans, insurance and taxes into one **Money Health Score** and a clear, prioritised action plan. Because users share this data with us, we know — with consent — their age, dependents, income, vehicle ownership and existing cover. That makes our insurance leads **high-intent and pre-qualified**.

## What we've already built

The full customer experience is live in-app, minus the regulated sale:

- **Needs discovery** — a guided check that tells users which cover types they may need.
- **Comparison UX** — real-plan catalogue with claim ratios, features and **indicative** premiums, ranked to the details the user enters.
- **Application flow** — captures the user's intent and details, ready to hand off.
- **Policy management** — encrypted policy locker, renewal reminders, coverage-gap analysis, plain-English glossary and a claims checklist.

## Compliance posture

PayWatch is a financial **education and organisation** tool (not a SEBI-registered adviser). On insurance we are deliberately conservative until partnered/licensed: premiums are shown as **indicative**, we **do not collect premium** or issue policies ourselves, wording avoids advice/recommendation, and every surface states that plans are "arranged through our IRDAI-licensed insurance partner, subject to insurer terms." We want your compliance to lead the regulated steps.
