PayWatch — Business Model Canvas

A one-page map of how PayWatch creates, delivers and captures value. Data-backed where research exists (Aug 2026).

Key Partners

  • Licensed insurance partner (Zopper / Riskcovry) → later IRDAI Corporate Agent
  • AMFI / BSE StAR MF infra (Cybrilla) for fund investing
  • Razorpay (payments), Supabase (infra)
  • Account Aggregator / FIU ecosystem (later)
  • MAHE Innovation Centre + XElevate (incubation, network)
  • Chartered Accountants — collaboration, never commission

Key Activities

  • Keep the computation engines correct & compliant
  • Continuous user research & validation
  • Customer-acquisition experiments
  • Regulatory sequencing (licences as milestones)
  • Trust & security

Key Resources

  • Proprietary deterministic engines (tax / score / ITR) = core IP
  • Integrated user financial data + data flywheel
  • RAG knowledge base + guardrails
  • Brand + founder domain expertise

Value Propositions

  • A private financial CFO in your pocket.
  • ONE Money Health Score + ONE plan across your whole financial life.
  • Trusted "what to do next," in plain language.
  • Neutral — never pushes a product.
  • Sees everything, so it can answer "Am I okay?" — the one thing single-vertical apps can't.

Customer Relationships

  • Self-serve app, low-touch
  • Proactive AI guidance (alerts, monthly briefing, Ask PayWatch)
  • Habit-forming — visible progress + nudges
  • Trust via transparency, bank-level security, no data-selling

Channels

  • iOS / Android apps + web PWA
  • Organic content & SEO (finance education)
  • Campus / community (Reddit, WhatsApp, colleges)
  • Referral / word-of-mouth; CA referrals
  • Primary paid channel = to be found via experiments

Customer Segments

  • Beachhead: young salaried earners 22–28, first 1–4 yrs of work, digitally native, anxious, no advisor
  • Expansion: working professionals 25–40 (families, EMIs)
  • Students with stipends (19–25)
  • 78% would use a financial-health app (research)

Cost Structure

  • Infra (hosting, DB, Claude API) + payment-gateway fees
  • Development (founder + interns / stipends)
  • Compliance & legal (incorporation, trademark, licences)
  • Customer acquisition — the big variable (CAC)
  • Philosophy: "lower costs, increased income" — lean, non-dilutive funding

Revenue Streams

  • Subscription: Free (anchor) · Starter ₹199/mo · CFO ₹499/mo
  • Distribution commission: mutual-fund trail (AMFI MFD) + insurance (licensed partner)
  • Never charge for advice (SEBI); monetise the user + distribution, never CAs
  • Status: designed, not yet validated — the #1 priority

PayWatch — Value Proposition Canvas

Left = what PayWatch offers. Right = what the user actually needs (from Vighnesh's empathy research, n=49 survey + 20 interviews). A good fit = the left relieves the right.

Value Map — what we offer

Products & Services
  • Money Health Score · ranked action plan · Ask PayWatch (AI on your data)
  • Tax computation + guided self-filing · insurance analysis + marketplace
  • Investment (category) guidance · net-worth + growth projection
  • Spending analysis · alerts & briefings · document vault · CA collaboration
Pain Relievers
  • One number tells you exactly where you stand → kills "don't know where I stand"
  • Ranked next-steps → kills decision paralysis / "where do I start?"
  • Plain-language beginner guidance → kills "acting without understanding"
  • Neutral + secure + transparent → addresses the trust gap
  • Proactive alerts → catches deadlines, cuts anxiety
Gain Creators
  • Single cross-silo view → the unified picture users ask for
  • Animated score + progress → makes progress visible
  • "What to do next" clarity · education-first → confidence
  • Life-stage aware → grows with them

Customer Profile — what they need

Customer Jobs
  • "Know if I'm on the right financial track"
  • "Know what to do next with my money"
  • "Handle tax / insurance / investing without becoming an expert"
  • "Feel in control — less money stress" · "Build an emergency fund"
Pains (from research)
  • "Don't know where to start" — 40% of 19–25
  • Decision paralysis — too many options
  • No emergency buffer — only 28.6% have one
  • Don't know whom to trust (parents / finfluencers / no one)
  • Stress even at higher income — 44.9% often/daily
  • Lifestyle inflation ("left with nothing") · tax & investing confusion
Gains
  • Clarity — "just tell me what to do" · simplicity
  • One unified view of all their money
  • Trusted guidance (they anchor trust on SEBI/RBI approval)
  • Independence, security, visible progress, confidence
Strategic tension to design around: the research shows users anchor trust on SEBI/RBI approval — the one badge PayWatch (education lane) won't have early. The value map must substitute for it deliberately: bank-level security, transparent "we never sell your data / never take commission to push products," and surfacing licensed partners' regulation. Trust is a build task, not a footnote.
